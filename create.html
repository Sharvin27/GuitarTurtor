Add a song

Type a song name. The helper finds a guitar cover on YouTube, transcribes the guitar and adds it to the tab view and the 3D hands.

Start the helper on your computer

Transcribing needs a small program running on your PC. GitHub Pages can only show the finished tabs.

  1. Open a terminal and run python D:\sharvin1\Projects\Music\songforge\server.py
  2. Open http://127.0.0.1:8777/create.html

Takes about 1–3 minutes. Automatic tabs are a starting point: roughly 3 in 4 notes right on clean guitar, fewer on distorted guitar over a backing track.
